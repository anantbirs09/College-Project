Gem Overview

Ruby (Manik)

Ruled by the Sun, ruby is associated with confidence, vitality, and strong personal presence. It is often recommended for those seeking courage, recognition, and a more commanding energy.

Leadership Vital Force Statement Stone

Why it stands out

The vivid red tone makes ruby one of the most striking traditional stones, often chosen by those drawn to visibility, strength, and authority.

Suggested mood

Ruby is often worn by people who want a stronger sense of confidence, leadership, and inner fire in daily life.

Care Ritual

Simple handling and cleansing guidance

Keep the gemstone clean with a soft cloth, avoid harsh exposure, and place it in a calm sacred space before first wear. A short morning prayer can reinforce the intentional, solar character of the stone.
